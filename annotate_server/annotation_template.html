Correct: conditioned score ≥ 0.8. Incorrect: conditioned score < 0.8. Exactly 0.8 is correct. Turns without a valid score are unscored.

Check the saved mtRAG explanation and statement checks against the evidence. Make your own annotation; a screening result alone does not establish a root cause.

Failure annotation guideline

First establish an observable error in the RAG answer. Compare selected context, gold documents, conversation, and corpus evidence. Review the gold answer independently too.

The 16 error types follow Leung et al., EACL 2026. Choose the earliest stage where evidence shows an error. Then choose one or more failure types in that stage. Earlier labels from other stages stay saved until you review them.

Original pre-rerank candidates and indexed chunk boundaries are not supplied by these conversation exports. Fresh corpus search does not reconstruct the original trace. Select Insufficient evidence when required traces are missing.

Other outcomes: use Not a failure for an acceptable answer, Outside taxonomy for an established error beyond the 16 error types, or Insufficient evidence when the cause remains unresolved. Outside taxonomy and Insufficient evidence are alternative outcomes, selected on their own.
