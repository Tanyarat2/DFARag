Judge guideline · mtRAG

Correct: conditioned score ≥ 0.8. Incorrect: conditioned score < 0.8. Exactly 0.8 is correct. Turns without a valid score are unscored.

Check the saved mtRAG explanation and statement checks against the evidence. Make your own annotation; a screening result alone does not establish a root cause.

Failure annotation guideline

First establish an observable error in the RAG answer. Compare selected context, gold documents, conversation, and corpus evidence. Review the gold answer independently too.

Select every failure cause supported by the original evidence. More than one cause can be selected; explain the evidence for each. Missing selected context alone cannot distinguish corpus scope, chunking, retrieval, and context selection. The conversation passage archive has limited coverage.

Other outcomes: use Not a failure for an acceptable answer, Outside taxonomy for an established error beyond the six causes, or Insufficient evidence when the cause remains unresolved. Outside taxonomy and Insufficient evidence are alternative outcomes, selected on their own.
